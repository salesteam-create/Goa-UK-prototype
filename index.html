Our story

Nearly six decades of community

Founded in 1966 and run entirely by volunteers, the Association keeps the language, traditions and togetherness of Goa alive — for every generation, from our elders to our youngest members.

What we do

Four ways we bring Goans together

🎭

Culture

Music, dance, Konkani language and the feasts that keep our heritage alive.

🤝

Community

Gatherings, socials and a network of friends who feel like family.

🎓

Youth

A youth wing helping young Goans connect with their roots and each other.

💛

Welfare

Support and a helping hand for members and newcomers settling in the UK.
